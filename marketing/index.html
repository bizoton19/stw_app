From check to claim.

Four beats at the table — snap, scan, share, settle among friends.

1 Snap

Load or snap the receipt

Camera or photo library. One shot of the check is enough — no typing the whole bill.

2 Scan

Line items appear

Vision reads the check into claimable lines. You skim a short list — not a spreadsheet dump — and confirm.

3 Share

QR with a claim link

A bright QR and a short URL for tonight’s tab. Guests open it in the browser — no app install.

4 Pass

Pass the phone — or send the link

Hand the QR around the table, or drop the link in the group chat. Friends claim what they ordered; tax and tip follow.
