How to invite

Share the claim link

One private link. How you deliver it depends on who’s at the table. Swipe the cards — source copy lives in FAQ.md.

1 · Mixed table

At the table

Best: QR

iPhones and Androids together. No contacts needed.

  1. Host taps QR on share
  2. Hold the code up or pass the phone
  3. Everyone scans with Camera
2 · Apple

iPhone → iPhone

Share → AirDrop

Not AirPlay. Both need AirDrop on (Everyone for 10 min works at dinner).

  1. Host taps Share
  2. Choose AirDrop
  3. Pick each person (often one by one)
3 · Cross

iPhone → Android

No nearby path

AirDrop will not reach Android. Use QR or a chat you already share.

  1. Show QR, or
  2. Share → Messages / WhatsApp, or
  3. Copy link
4 · Google

Android → Android

Share → Nearby

Nearby Share / Quick Share (name varies by brand). QR if Nearby flakes.

  1. Host taps Share
  2. Nearby / Quick Share
  3. Pick the nearby phone
5 · Cross

Android → iPhone

No nearby path

Nearby will not reach iPhone. Same as the other cross case: QR or chat.

  1. Show QR, or
  2. Share into WhatsApp / Messages, or
  3. Copy link

Product rule: Share opens the OS sheet. Split the Wine does not invent a cross-platform nearby stack — QR is the universal table path.
