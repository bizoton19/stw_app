# Marketing Strategy: Split the Wine

*Built for the table, not the ledger.*

## Positioning
This isn't a "fintech" app or a social network for spending. It is a **low-friction utility** designed to solve the specific, awkward moment at the end of a group dinner.

**The Core Angle:**
We don't want your data. We don't want your friends list. We just want to do the math for you.

## Marketing Scenarios & Steps

### Scenario 1: The Group Chat Drop (Organic Viral)
The product *is* the marketing. You don't market to the guests; you market to the "host" (the person who always puts their card down).
1. **The Hook:** Target the designated "card putter-downer" in friend groups.
2. **The Action:** They snap the receipt and drop the link in iMessage/WhatsApp.
3. **The Magic:** Guests click the link. They don't download an app. They don't create an account. They just tap the wine they drank.
4. **The Viral Loop:** Guests experience how painless it was, and remember to download the app when *they* are the host next time.

### Scenario 2: The 15-Second TikTok / Reels Demo
Show, don't tell. The UI is clean and fast enough to demo in a short video.
1. **0:00 - 0:03:** Messy table, long receipt. Audio: "Splitting this used to ruin the end of dinner."
2. **0:03 - 0:08:** App scans receipt instantly. "Snap the check."
3. **0:08 - 0:11:** Screen record dropping the link in a group chat. "Drop the link."
4. **0:11 - 0:15:** Split screen: 4 friends tapping their items on their phones. "Everyone claims what they drank, math is done automatically."

### Scenario 3: The Privacy/Ephemeral Flex
Position against bloated finance apps that want to be social networks.
1. **Copy:** "Why does your expense app need to read your contacts? We don't. You already have a group chat."
2. **Highlight:** "Tabs expire. We don't process your payments. We just do the math and open Venmo for you."

## Key Messaging Principles (K.I.S.S.)

*   **Fair, not equal:** Tax and tip follow what you actually ordered. No more splitting the $100 steak equally with the person who ordered a side salad.
*   **Host stays host:** The money goes directly to your Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, PayPal, MonCash, or Natcash.
*   **No Guest Apps:** The biggest friction killer. Only one person needs the app.
*   **Ephemeral:** The link lives for tonight. It's not a permanent ledger of your life.
