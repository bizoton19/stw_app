Card design + theme review — first pass

Bespoke SVG motifs, four themes, and the three card surfaces that carry them. Nothing here is wired into the app yet.

Motif sources live in plans/motifs/*.svg. Plan: plans/card-design-themes.md.

Override + rollback. linen is the exact current contract from plans/ui-enhance.guide.md §3 and is the default. candlelight, cellar, and patio intentionally break the “one accent, no new palette” law you approved overriding. Three of them reuse hexes that already exist in apps/mobile/src/lib/theme.ts (merlot, kindFood amber, select green), so rollback is deleting a theme entry — not unpicking a redesign.

3 · Where Three.js earns its place (and where it doesn’t)

SurfaceVerdictWhy
Marketing heroYesDesktop, no battery pressure, no claim deadline. Best showcase for a real scene.
Settle “You owe”Maybe — one pourSingle emotional beat. A 1.5s liquid fill reading the theme accent. Must have a static SVG fallback.
Share cardNot v1The win here is a crisp, screenshot-worthy SVG card. WebGL adds load time to the step where the host is rushing.
Claim rowsNoScroll performance and thumb latency at a dim table. 2D forever.
Native (Expo)DeferR3F on RN needs expo-gl + native modules; breaks the Expo-Go-compatible constraint.
